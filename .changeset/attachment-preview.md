Show attachment previews in the chat. Files sent with a message now appear in
the conversation next to the user's bubble — images as thumbnails you can open
full-size, other files as a labelled chip — instead of vanishing after send.
Attachment-only messages render just the preview (no filename placeholder text).
Previews are in-session only, consistent with attachments never being persisted.
